pocket drama

app design [ nov’ 25 - Jan’ 26 ]
pocket drama [the name of the app has been changed] is an entertainig app featuring mini-dramas and other short content.
I designed the MVP version of the app as part of a small product team of 6. Specifically, I was responsible for building a general user flow, conducting basic competitor benchmarking, creating low and high-fidelity prototypes, developing the app's visual concept, designing UI kit components, and preparing the developer handoff.
As the client strongly insisted on their own visual concept, having rejected several of our proposals, I decided to showcase the design direction I would have personally chosen.
